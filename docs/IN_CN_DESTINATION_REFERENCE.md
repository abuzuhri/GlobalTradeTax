# India and China destination references

`GET /v1/references/national/{country}` provides authenticated, tenant-budgeted static source pointers for IN and CN. Two ASCII letters are accepted case-insensitively; malformed codes return 400 and unsupported codes return 404. No other country or customs territory is covered. The response is `reference_only`, its territory is `unresolved`, monetary amounts are null, and calculation, payment and shipment-release approval are false. Destination routing remains `provider_required`. Source review on 2026-11-09 is not a legal effective date. No live request or provider is used.

India: [CBIC Customs Act section 46](https://taxinformation.cbic.gov.in/content-page/explore-act/1000086/1000002) addresses import entry and restrictions; [CBIC integrated tax legislation](https://taxinformation.cbic.gov.in/content-page/explore-act/1000613/1000001) addresses tax on imported goods; [DGFT foreign trade policy chapter 2](https://content.dgft.gov.in/Website/dgftprod/9158887c-cdfb-4312-92f1-15eeb8e8aa70/%5BUPDATED%5D%20CHAPTER%202%20OF%20FTP.pdf) discusses classification, import policy, IEC and documentation. Check amendments and current applicable provisions.

China: the [State Taxation Administration tariff law](https://fgk.chinatax.gov.cn/zcfgk/c100009/c5234556/content.html) addresses customs duties; the State Council's [2024 foreign investment guide](https://english.www.gov.cn/AssetsZi/Foreign_Investment_Guide_of_the_People%27s_Republic_of_China_2024.pdf), section 5.4, describes commercial customs declarations and clearance; the [VAT law](https://tianjin.chinatax.gov.cn/11200000000/0300/030004/03000418/p20241226144544917.shtml) addresses imported goods; [customs administrative penalty regulations](https://english.www.gov.cn/services/doingbusiness/202102/24/content_WS6035f3c3c6d0719374af97b8.html) address controls and licensing enforcement, not a complete control catalogue. Verify current legislation and applicable catalogues. A CN code alone does not determine applicability within a distinct customs territory; Hong Kong, Macao and Taiwan are outside this endpoint's coverage.

For each shipment independently verify classification, actual origin and preference evidence, valuation, importer registration and broker, import regime, duties, taxes and fees, product permits and prohibitions, special zones and precise territorial scope, and postal, courier or low-value applicability. These links do not establish reuse rights, rates, exemptions, eligibility, liability or clearance. Live effective-dated data, source licensing, independent tax and legal review, and other TASK-011 acceptance criteria remain open.

The CBIC legislation pages were reviewed through indexed official content on 2026-11-09; direct page opening timed out or returned an error during this review. Recheck availability and amendments before relying on them.
