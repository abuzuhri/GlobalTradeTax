# EU destination reference guidance — TASK-011

`GET /v1/references/eu/destinations/{country}` returns authenticated, rate-limited source guidance for the 27 EU member-state ISO alpha-2 codes, using a membership snapshot reviewed on 2026-11-08. Two ASCII letters are accepted case-insensitively; malformed codes return 400 and non-member or unknown codes return 404. For example, authenticated `GET /v1/references/eu/destinations/pl` returns `PL`, `Poland`, `reference_only`, `territoryStatus: unresolved`, `verifiedForCalculation: false`, and `referenceReviewedOn: 2026-11-08`. The review date is not a legal effective date. No tariff, VAT rate, tax total or entitlement is returned. This endpoint performs no network call and does not offer historical membership or effective-dated tariff data.

The result identifies distinct checks and official source pages for the exact territory, EU TARIC, and national VAT. [European Commission territorial scope](https://taxation-customs.ec.europa.eu/taxation/vat/vat-directive/how-does-vat-work/territorial-scope_en) shows why a member-state country code alone is insufficient: for example, Åland and the Canary Islands have special VAT treatment. Cyprus and other special territories require their own review. This API does not determine whether an individual address is in or out of a customs or VAT territory; it does not infer exemptions, relief or free circulation.

[EU TARIC](https://taxation-customs.ec.europa.eu/online-services/online-services-and-databases-customs/eu-customs-tariff-taric_en) is an official tariff reference but does not supply national VAT or excise rates. The Commission's [country-specific VAT information](https://taxation-customs.ec.europa.eu/taxation/vat/vat-directive/vat-rates/country-specific-information-vat_en) points to member-state sources. These links are guidance, not ingested tariff data, independently verified legal applicability, or authority to charge a merchant. Source rights, source contracts, effective dates, origin, classification, legal conditions, customs value, procedure, national VAT and independently approved case evidence remain prerequisites for payable calculation. GCC, US, XI and other non-EU-member codes are not covered by this endpoint.

The existing quote and landed-cost gates remain in force. The EU compliance reminder now covers all 27 member codes rather than six, without deciding VAT or IOSS eligibility. Existing destination policy responses remain `provider_required`; the static source guidance does not configure a provider or enable settlement. Exact territory, goods location, goods status, registration, transaction facts and the applicable date must be established independently. The membership snapshot must be maintained against authoritative sources if membership changes.

The Release tests exercise all 27 member codes, case normalization, unsupported and invalid inputs, unresolved territory, source separation, non-payable quote fields, compliance reminders and response isolation. The disposable container suite covers authentication and actual HTTP responses; PR checks contain per-commit execution evidence. TASK-011 remains in progress until official-source cases, data rights and legal applicability are verified.
