## MY, ID, TH, VN and PH commercial import review (2026-11-09)

The date records inspection, not legal effect. These pointers are not comprehensive, effective-dated datasets or permission to reuse data. Verify current law and shipment facts with the responsible authorities.

- **Malaysia:** Royal Malaysian Customs Department [customs legislation index](https://www.customs.gov.my/en/procedure/customs/customs) lists the Customs Act, regulations and orders and says the official Laws of Malaysia portal prevails. The previously linked Act PDF returned HTTP 404 on direct retrieval on 2026-11-09; the index is a discovery aid, not verification of current statutory text or applicability; its [sales tax FAQ](https://mysst.customs.gov.my/faq-sales-tax/) describes import sales tax and designated/special areas. The [import prohibition amendment](https://www.customs.gov.my/images/06-prosedur/perintah-kastam/larangan-import/P.U.A295-perintah_kastam_larangan_mengenai_import_pindaan_2023.pdf) is an amendment, not the full current controlled-goods list; verify the principal order and subsequent amendments.
- **Indonesia:** Directorate General of Customs and Excise [import for consumption](https://www.beacukai.go.id/impor-untuk-dipakai) discusses declarations, import duties and taxes, restrictions and exclusions for certain procedures and zones. Its [restrictions guidance](https://www.beacukai.go.id/larangan-pembatasan) directs commodity checks to the national single window.
- **Thailand:** Laem Chabang Port Customs Office [commercial import procedures](https://lcpcb.customs.go.th/th/import-procedures) discusses declarations, duties and permits. It is port guidance; verify current national tariff, VAT and product-specific requirements separately.
- **Vietnam:** Vietnam Customs [procedure index](https://pus.customs.gov.vn/faces/Procedure) lists import and tax procedures in the search index; direct retrieval timed out during review. The accessible official [customs circular](https://files.customs.gov.vn/CustomsCMS/DONG_NAI/2025/12/26/121_2025_TT_BTC_18_12_2025.pdf) discusses import declarations and special zones. Current import tax and product-control rules could not be independently verified comprehensively from these materials and remain an explicit open check.
- **Philippines:** Bureau of Customs [CMTA import clearance text](https://customs.gov.ph/wp-content/uploads/2023/01/CMTA-RA-10863-2.pdf) covers entry, duties and taxes. Its [commodity documentation FAQ](https://client.customs.gov.ph/kb/faq.php?id=12) mentions agency clearance for regulated goods; verify current requirements with each regulator.

Country codes do not establish customs territory, origin, preference, low-value eligibility or any rate. Classification, valuation, importer registration and broker responsibility, regime, fees, permits and special-zone treatment require shipment-specific review. No calculation, payment or release is approved. Independent legal and data-rights review and a live effective-dated provider remain open.
## US, Canada and Mexico references (reviewed 2026-11-09)

- US: [USITC HTS](https://hts.usitc.gov/) publishes the tariff; [CBP](https://www.help.cbp.gov/s/article/Article-1397) describes import classification and controls. [IRS educational material](https://apps.irs.gov/app/understandingTaxes/whys/thm04/les01/media/is1_thm04_les01.pdf) distinguishes federal, state and local tax; consult the actual destination jurisdiction for sales/use tax.
- Canada: [CBSA tariff](https://www.cbsa-asfc.gc.ca/trade-commerce/tariff-tarif/2026/menu-eng.html) and [origin guidance](https://www.cbsa-asfc.gc.ca/import/origin-origine-eng.html); [CRA place-of-supply guidance](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/charge-collect-place-supply.html). Import and provincial taxes require separate fact-specific review.
- Mexico: [SAT classification and NICO](https://wwwmat.sat.gob.mx/tramites/71719/presenta-tu-consulta-de-clasificacion-arancelaria), [ANAM customs guidance](https://aduanafacil.anam.gob.mx/) and [SAT import VAT valuation](https://wwwmatnp.sat.gob.mx/articulo/43379/articulo-27). ANAM guidance includes passenger-specific material and does not establish commercial procedure.

Review dates record inspection, not legal effective dates or shipment applicability. Check importer, actual origin, classification, valuation, controls, fees, procedures, postal/courier facts, exact territory and subnational tax. Preferential treatment requires specific origin and proof; membership or shipping country alone is insufficient. No rates or thresholds are supplied, and source links do not establish data rights or legal eligibility.
