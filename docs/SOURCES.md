## MY, ID, TH, VN and PH commercial import review (2026-11-09)

The date records inspection, not legal effect. These pointers are not comprehensive, effective-dated datasets or permission to reuse data. Verify current law and shipment facts with the responsible authorities.

- **Malaysia:** Royal Malaysian Customs Department [customs legislation index](https://www.customs.gov.my/en/procedure/customs/customs) lists the Customs Act, regulations and orders and says the official Laws of Malaysia portal prevails. The previously linked Act PDF returned HTTP 404 on direct retrieval on 2026-11-09; the index is a discovery aid, not verification of current statutory text or applicability; its [sales tax FAQ](https://mysst.customs.gov.my/faq-sales-tax/) describes import sales tax and designated/special areas. The [import prohibition amendment](https://www.customs.gov.my/images/06-prosedur/perintah-kastam/larangan-import/P.U.A295-perintah_kastam_larangan_mengenai_import_pindaan_2023.pdf) is an amendment, not the full current controlled-goods list; verify the principal order and subsequent amendments.
- **Indonesia:** Directorate General of Customs and Excise [import for consumption](https://www.beacukai.go.id/impor-untuk-dipakai) discusses declarations, import duties and taxes, restrictions and exclusions for certain procedures and zones. Its [restrictions guidance](https://www.beacukai.go.id/larangan-pembatasan) directs commodity checks to the national single window.
- **Thailand:** Laem Chabang Port Customs Office [commercial import procedures](https://lcpcb.customs.go.th/th/import-procedures) discusses declarations, duties and permits. It is port guidance; verify current national tariff, VAT and product-specific requirements separately.
- **Vietnam:** Vietnam Customs [procedure index](https://pus.customs.gov.vn/faces/Procedure) lists import and tax procedures in the search index; direct retrieval timed out during review. The accessible official [customs circular](https://files.customs.gov.vn/CustomsCMS/DONG_NAI/2025/12/26/121_2025_TT_BTC_18_12_2025.pdf) discusses import declarations and special zones. Current import tax and product-control rules could not be independently verified comprehensively from these materials and remain an explicit open check.
- **Philippines:** Bureau of Customs [CMTA import clearance text](https://customs.gov.ph/wp-content/uploads/2023/01/CMTA-RA-10863-2.pdf) covers entry, duties and taxes. Its [commodity documentation FAQ](https://client.customs.gov.ph/kb/faq.php?id=12) mentions agency clearance for regulated goods; verify current requirements with each regulator.

Country codes do not establish customs territory, origin, preference, low-value eligibility or any rate. Classification, valuation, importer registration and broker responsibility, regime, fees, permits and special-zone treatment require shipment-specific review. No calculation, payment or release is approved. Independent legal and data-rights review and a live effective-dated provider remain open.

## Argentina, Uruguay and Paraguay commercial-import reference (reviewed 2026-11-09)

These pointers provide context, not comprehensive verified commercial-import guidance or effective-dated data. Review dates are not legal effective dates.

- Argentina: [ARCA customs code](https://biblioteca.arca.gob.ar/dcp/LEY_C_022415_1981_03_02) includes importer and customs provisions; [ARCA international shipments](https://www.arca.gob.ar/envios-internacionales/regimen-general/) explains when shipments fall outside postal/courier regimes. The latter is shipment-specific, not a complete commercial import guide.
- Uruguay: [customs import tax context](https://www.aduanas.gub.uy/innovaportal/v/2755/8/innova.front/regimen-impositivo-a-la-importacion-.html), [digital declaration procedure](https://www.aduanas.gub.uy/innovaportal/file/10163/1/procedimiento_dua_digital_-_importacion.pdf), and [special border trade restrictions](https://www.aduanas.gub.uy/innovaportal/v/28040/1/innova.front/). The procedural PDF is older and its current applicability must be checked; border rules are limited to that regime.
- Paraguay: DNIT indexes for [customs actor registration](https://ekuatia.set.gov.py/es/web/portal-institucional/personas-vinculadas-a-la-actividad-aduanera) and [VUI import permits](https://www.dnit.gov.py/en/web/portal-institucional/vui); [MIC selected product procedures](https://www.mic.gov.py/tramites-para-importaciones/) is not exhaustive. Confirm current tariff and tax treatment separately.

For each shipment verify classification, actual origin and preference evidence, value, importer and broker obligations, regime, taxes and fees, permits and prohibitions, postal/courier eligibility, exact territory and special zones. Regional or agreement membership establishes none of these. Rights, live data and independent legal approval remain open.

## NO, CH and IS commercial-import reference sources (inspected 2026-11-12)

These are discovery pointers, not licensed, effective-dated tariff data or verified shipment decisions. Inspection date is not a legal effective date. No reuse rights have been established.

- Norway: [Norwegian Customs import guide for businesses](https://www.toll.no/en/corporate/import/import-guide-for-beginners) discusses classification, import declaration, VAT and other duties, restrictions and the customs treatment of Svalbard, Jan Mayen and dependencies. It is general guidance, not a shipment ruling.
- Switzerland: the Federal Office for Customs and Border Security's [commercial import checklist](https://www.bazg.admin.ch/en/importation-checklist-companies), [import tax overview](https://www.bazg.admin.ch/en/value-added-tax-and-import-tax-companies), and [goods traffic restrictions](https://www.bazg.admin.ch/en/bans-restrictions-conditions-goods-traffic) cover declarations, tax context and controlled goods. Its [trade statistics territory description](https://www.bazg.admin.ch/en/swiss-foreign-trade-statistics-methods-survey) identifies territorial differences; statistical scope alone does not decide tax applicability. Liechtenstein is separately coded and unsupported by this endpoint.
- Iceland: Iceland Revenue and Customs' [company import overview](https://www.skatturinn.is/english/companies/customs-matters/importing-to-iceland/) covers procedures and import charges, and its [import documents page](https://www.skatturinn.is/english/companies/customs-matters/importing-to-iceland/import-documents/) discusses origin evidence and licences where required. The English overview itself points to more detailed Icelandic information; it is not a comprehensive catalogue of import controls. Check the relevant competent authority for each product.

For all three, verify classification, actual goods origin and preferential proof, valuation, importer registration and broker, regime, duties, taxes and fees, permits and prohibitions, postal/courier/low-value applicability and exact territory and zone for each shipment. Country code alone establishes none of these. Live authoritative integration, source rights and independent tax/legal review remain open.
